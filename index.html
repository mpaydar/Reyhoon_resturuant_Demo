Rayhoon — Persian for “basil”

Two brothers, one table.

Rayhoon is the work of brothers Mehdi and Reza Kazerani, who brought their love of food and hospitality together under one roof.

Mehdi spent more than 20 years alongside Chef Peter Loria and Larry Falcone at Café Matisse, a Michelin-nominated, Zagat-rated room once named among America's top 100 restaurants. Reza brings his culinary training from the prestigious Hotel Espinas in Iran, along with the recipes of home.
